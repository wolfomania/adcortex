# Bundled data

| File | Content | Source |
|------|---------|--------|
| `atlas_fsaverage5.json` | HCP-MMP1.0 parcel per fsaverage5 vertex (20,484), parcel long names, Glasser section | Generated by `scripts/export_brain_assets.py` |
| `systems.yaml` | The 10 brain systems: parcels, description, confidence, citations | Hand-written; see `docs/science.md` |
| `baseline.json` | Reference statistics from 9 Instagram posts with sound: each system's per-second mean and SD, each clip's mean and Hook, each vertex's mean and SD | Generated by `scripts/build_baseline.py` from `baseline/sources.yaml`; derived from TRIBE v2 output, so its CC BY-NC 4.0 license applies |
| `replay_v1.npz` | One real TRIBE v2 prediction (17 s of an event highlights video, all 20,484 vertices, no transcript) for `ADCORTEX_BRAIN_BACKEND=replay` | Recorded by `scripts/record_replay_fixture.py`; a TRIBE v2 output, so its CC BY-NC 4.0 license applies |

## HCP-MMP1.0 terms

`atlas_fsaverage5.json` and `frontend/public/brain/atlas.json` are derived from
the HCP-MMP1.0 parcellation (Glasser et al., 2016, Nature 536:171–178),
projected to fsaverage (figshare 3498446). They are redistributed under the
WU-Minn HCP Open Access Data Use Terms. Acknowledgment required by those terms:

> Data were provided [in part] by the Human Connectome Project, WU-Minn
> Consortium (Principal Investigators: David Van Essen and Kamil Ugurbil;
> 1U54MH091657) funded by the 16 NIH Institutes and Centers that support the
> NIH Blueprint for Neuroscience Research; and by the McDonnell Center for
> Systems Neuroscience at Washington University.

Parcel long names come from the HCPex abbreviation table distributed with Lead-DBS
(`ext_libs/dsi_studio/atlas/human/HCPex_abbreviations.txt`).
